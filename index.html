I am a postdoctoral researcher in the School of Electrical Engineering at KAIST, where I received my Ph.D. in August 2026 under the supervision of Prof. Yongdae Kim. My research interests lie in the security of mobile communication systems, including LTE, 5G, and 6G networks as well as satellite-based non-terrestrial networks (NTN). I build testing frameworks that find vulnerabilities in real networks and devices, from device basebands to operator core networks. One of them, CITesting, received the Distinguished Paper Award at ACM CCS 2025. I am also exploring how large language models can support protocol analysis and security testing. My work has been published at ACM CCS, USENIX Security, NDSS, and ACM WiSec, and I have led security assessment projects with SK Telecom, LG Uplus, and national 5G/6G security programs.

  • Cellular network security (LTE/5G/6G)
  • Baseband security
  • Wireless system security
  • Fuzzing and testing frameworks
  • LLM-assisted protocol analysis
